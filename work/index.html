  • Launched an AI-powered chatbot that handles 36% of diner chat contacts end to end (1.5M a year) and took customer-experience automation from 55% to 80%, saving $4.5M annually; added voice AI in several business lines.
  • Drove AI-assisted engineering adoption from 25% to 95% weekly usage, applying agentic workflows to raise test coverage from 70% to 95%, remediate critical vulnerabilities and retire legacy systems.
  • Modernized Post Purchase on a backend-for-frontend, server-driven architecture, cutting $2M+ in annual costs while improving engineering velocity by 50%.
